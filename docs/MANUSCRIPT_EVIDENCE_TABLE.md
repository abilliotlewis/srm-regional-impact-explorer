# Manuscript evidence table

## Scope and use

This table supports a manuscript framed as **Regional robustness and uncertainty of G6solar and G6sulfur climate responses over the Southeast United States**. Numerical wording is tied to committed outputs. Values are G6solar minus G6sulfur unless stated otherwise. Temperature is in degrees Celsius and precipitation is in millimeters per day. The compact, machine-readable source for the main numerical claims is `docs/MANUSCRIPT_KEY_RESULTS.csv`.

## Claims that should not be made

| Unsafe claim | Why it is unsupported | Safer alternative |
|---|---|---|
| “G6solar is more effective than G6sulfur.” | Effectiveness requires an explicit objective and broader climate, impact, feasibility, and risk criteria. | “G6solar produces lower JJA regional `tasmax` than G6sulfur in this four-model, matched-experiment comparison.” |
| “SRM would cool the Southeast by X degrees Celsius.” | The main result is G6solar minus G6sulfur, not intervention minus an observed or no-SRM baseline. | “The ensemble-mean G6solar-minus-G6sulfur JJA difference is X degrees Celsius for the specified domain.” |
| “Four models prove a robust consensus.” | Four equally weighted models are a limited structural sample. | “All four included model means have the same sign, with the stated leave-one-out, spatial, and temporal checks.” |
| “Bootstrap intervals quantify GeoMIP uncertainty.” | They quantify temporal sampling within individual simulations, conditional on nominal-year alignment and block-resampling assumptions. | “Five-year moving-block intervals characterize temporal-sampling variability within each model simulation.” |
| “Nominal-year pairing creates synchronized weather differences.” | Calendar-year labels do not synchronize internal weather across independent scenario integrations. | “Experiments were aligned by nominal seasonal year to form a reproducible difference series.” |
| “The precipitation response is robust.” | Signs, intervals, spatial agreement, and geographic sensitivity are mixed. | “Precipitation ordering is weaker and more geographically and structurally sensitive than the JJA temperature ordering.” |
| “The intervals show statistical significance.” | The bootstrap is a sensitivity framework with simulation-specific and pairing assumptions, not a complete inferential model. | “The interval excludes/includes zero under the stated moving-block procedure.” |
| “The results resolve local or county-scale impacts.” | Model grids and broad masks cannot support that precision. | “Results characterize broad regional and subregional averages and one-degree ensemble-map patterns.” |
| “Daily extremes confirm the four-model result.” | Daily-extreme results use only MPI-ESM1-2-LR `r2i1p1f1`. | “The MPI single-model case study provides supplementary process-relevant context.” |
